# Game manual

What the printed instruction booklet says about the mechanics.

## Map screen

- The map screen is opened with SELECT.
- A "!?" mark on the map shows important locations you need to visit to proceed through the game.
- A "message" mark on the map marks a location where you heard an important message. Press A there to make the message appear again.

## Actions

- Pushing: stand next to a stone or statue-shaped object and hold a direction into it. Sometimes objects are hidden under stones, and a movable stone may open a dungeon door.
- Swimming and diving: press A to swim, B to dive. Suspicious places in the water may hide something.
- Pegasus Boots: running into a wall or tree at speed can break through it or shake loose a hidden object.
- Shield: bring it into a ready position by holding its assigned button. It repels most enemy attacks, not all of them.
- Long messages: if a message is very long, press A to see all of it.

## General rules

- There are 8 dungeons. Each is guarded by a Nightmare boss holding one of 8 musical instruments, and each needs its own key to enter.
- Some treasure chests open on sight; others only appear after all monsters in the room are defeated.
- Collecting 4 Heart Pieces gives 1 more Heart Container; there are 12 Heart Pieces in all. Link starts with 3 Heart Containers.

# Loose ends and measured limits (moved out of the roadmap, 2026-11-05)

Details behind the one-line items of `ROADMAP.md`: what was measured, in which run, and what stays unmeasured. Update an entry when a limit is measured or fixed; delete it when it is gone.

- **Inventory and shield.** Item ids other than the shield (4) and the sword (1, verified on 2 states of run 20261111-142812-34ca, then live in 20261111-194737-9ba1) are general-knowledge hypotheses in `data/items.json` (shown with a `?`); item counts (bombs, powder), upgrades (shield or sword level) and secondary items outside the 10 slots are not read, and an item regained after being used up is not re-emitted; 0xDB47 changes over time (unexplained). `use_item` holds the button; edge-triggered items, items that need aiming and the effect of an item are unmeasured; A and B are ignored 16 frames after the menu closes (measured in 2 states). `guard` is measured without enemies: `shield_raised` (0xC15B) is 1 while the button is held, Link walks at full speed and turns freely; what it blocks is not measured. Live run `20261102-152517-ad15` (flow-17, 1 USD, 19,897 frames, 53 calls, 27 places): `item_gained` shield fired once at frame 4661 and the context showed it on B in every later prompt, but the agent never chose `use_item` or `guard` (no enemy met), so neither is confirmed live.

- **People and dialogues.** A text is attributed to the entity `talk_to` faced when the dialogue opened, else to the last successful `talk_to` target cell (a text that opens by itself is not attributed); two people of the same entity type in a place share one memory; a dialogue longer than the page limit counts as several talks; the world stamp (milestone, items held, high and normal leads) misses other changes (an event, a time of day); a person cycling through older texts counts as exhausted after two talks with nothing new, so a text only given on a third round is missed; `new_text` and people's memory compare letters and digits only; stitching joins on an overlap of 7+ characters that starts on a word and may end mid-word, so a real repeated phrase of that length across two pages is merged once; the conversation signature (sorted full text pixel rows of the box, found as rows filled at 90 % with the darkest DMG shade) is not checked in dark rooms or with a yes/no box; type 117 `npc` is verified once; `creature` types 61 and 220 are measured once (45 and 108-110 at least twice); type 47 (162/0) stays uncatalogued (the census saw contact damage with and without its slot) and still gates the revisit when it moves within 3 cells; sprites without an entity (3 outdoors in 161/0) are unexplained.

- **Place cards.** Exits are border cells believed walkable, not exits taken; a card is written per visit, not refreshed when entities move or appear later in the visit.
- **Doors.** Door ids are measured on map 0 only (226 at 5 house doors, verified; 225 at one door, hypothesis); doors entered from another side than below, stairs, cave mouths and dungeon entrances are not measured; a door whose cell below is not reachable is never tried. - **Leads.** Sheets are attached only to `choose_skill`, so an objective chosen right after a dialogue cannot cite its leads yet; a failed sheet call loses the dialogue pages (PNGs remain under `prompts/`).

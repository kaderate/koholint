## Next, in order

2. **Autonomous improvement loop toward the first dungeon (owner gave full autonomy).** Observe a run, read its symptoms (`summary.json` already counts `left_with_unexhausted_people`, `damage_events`, `deaths`, `stuck_events`; still to add: not ready, partial maps, sign unread, unused item, repeated failure, cost drift), decide harness change versus longer sessions, fix, relaunch (1-10 USD); then an offline analyst agent writing proposals (`koholint analyze <run>`). Level 3 (patch in a worktree, tests, keyframe-forked A/B) later. The agent may write more data, never code. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-screen-protocol-and-improvement-loop-design.md`.
3. **Capability learning loop (brief: `docs/research/AUTO_IMPROVEMENT_BRIEF.md`, design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-capability-learning-slice.md`).** `koholint learn` proposes, tries and judges capabilities offline; `capability publish|retire` moves them in and out of play (`data/capabilities.json`, digest in each run's manifest); `sword-on-object-92` (the sword on room object `0x5C`) is operational and offered while its object is on screen. The use-based A/B from `sword-obtained` (run on flow-39 code, 3 forks per arm, 6000 frames, 5.04 USD) failed: offered in 45 of 117 skill prompts of the capabilities arm, chosen 0 times; both arms only revisited the parent's 42 places (no new place or item, and the milestone ladder tops out at the sword). flow-43 feeds operational "clears object" capabilities into explore, goto and the planner; a scripted explore from checkpoint `clearable-objects-160` cleared three `0x5C` cells and reached 164/17, new to the lineage, but the agent-driven A/B from there (tag `cap43`, 5.87 USD) had 0 uses and 0 new places in both arms. Each fork's first objective (a person in 161/0) pulled explore through a known exit, and the forks then looped between known places, "revisit npc" rounds and `no_frontier`. flow-44 takes clearable frontiers before an aimed route, makes a detected loop abandon the objective and explore toward untried work, and holds NPC revisits while a surveyed place has untried work. The cap44 A/B from `clearable-objects-160` passed: all 3 capabilities forks reached 164/17 (one also 128/0) at 0.084 USD per 1000 frames, against no new place at 0.159 for the forks without. Next: a better ranking of uncatalogued object ids: blocking evidence pooled over `0c64`'s 32-run lineage ranks `0x5C` 8th (5 blocked cells), behind `0x1F`, `0x48`, `0x49` and `0x60`. Keep a narrow MetaPlanner for harness defects only. Measure game progress per 1000 frames and per dollar against a baseline fork, not the number of skills.

## Open questions and unmeasured limits (one line each, details in `docs/loose-ends.md`)
- Zero-frame cap (flow-45): its thresholds are not measured: 3 identical results, at most 1 frame each, then a 120-frame wait. The key is place + skill + stop, so a model alternating two instant skills never trips it.
- Heal sources: no heart was picked up in the attack demos and enemy drops after a kill are not watched; hp stays 8/24 or lower.
- Does entering a house or cave heal? Not observed (hp stayed 4/24 in two houses for 2600 frames; caves untested): the `shelter` candidate was removed in flow-34 (no retreat at low hp).
- Routes over known places: an exit reachable from one landing of a place is assumed reachable from every landing except a pocket cell (one landing cell kept per side); in the scripted run from `sword-obtained` (flow-39) Link reached 192 in 1837 frames, found 192 up to 176 is a ledge, and then had no known route to 177-0 and no untried exit within reach (the bushes the sword may cut and the urchins are not frontiers yet).
- Loop watch (4 entries of a known place in 3600 frames without a new place) is measured on run `4ec5` only; on 21 older runs it would fire in 10 (mostly two screens crossed back and forth), whether all of them were loops is unchecked. The 225-0 contradiction of `4ec5` (row 4, col 5, up, blocked twice) is Link 4 px right of the cell centre beside the urchin of (3,6), not a map error (not replayed).
- Unmeasured thresholds: third of max hp, 3-cell danger range, 6-result stuck count, revisit caps (1800 / 2700 frames, 6 rounds, 3 stamps), autopilot chain caps (6 explores, 2400 frames), `untried_object` range, 2 failed objectives to refute a lead; a lead is `tested` when an objective that cited it was achieved, which says the objective was reached, not that the claim was true.
- `gap_entry` (-3..+4 px) is measured on one stairs cell; the escape from the 165/16 pocket is not replayed end to end.
- Addresses at `verified_count: 1` (`name_length`, `max_hearts`, `dialog_state`, `shield_box`, `gap_entry`, `link_frozen`); `entity_state` (0xC290) and 0xDB47 are unexplained; item ids other than 4, counts and upgrades are unread.
- Whether a run may inherit a map, a goal or leads from a previous run; ladder beyond the sword (goal text only, no milestone yet).
- People and dialogues: speaker attribution is a guess when two people overlap; stitching of scrolling pages and the exhaustion rule can miss a text given only on a third round; creature types 61, 220 and 47 are not settled; type 111 walks off its cell.
- Doors, signs, readiness, game modes and place kinds are measured on the overworld and houses only (caves, dungeons, holes, swimming, stairs are not); scripted scenes (`link_frozen`) are measured on the two scenes of 242/0 only.
- 0xC167 is unexplained: 15 through the whole sword scene, but 56 for 3646 frames of the intro (Link free at the end) and 1 for 95 frames in room 194 at frame 36951 of run 9ba1 with Link still and no other lock signal (not replayed); the 30-frame wait after a long lock rests on one 3-frame release.
- The `scene` stop applies to every skill: a scene type not measured yet (chests, holes, stairs, other characters arriving) that holds Link could cut `explore` or `goto` short; `link_frozen` and the `frozen` mode rest on one run and one room (9ba1, 242/0) until another confirms them.
- Explore memory (walls, tried exits, sides) is per place and permanent; border exits are tried blindly; a harness-picked `new_place` objective skips the model's first look.
- Agent loop: timed-out client calls count as $0; `Run#fork` assumes older prefixes are compatible and drops the events of the fork frame (a branch at `sword-obtained` has no sword `item_gained`; the milestone still reads from the state); `verify` replays from boot; viewer host check on GET routes only.

# Roadmap — what remains

Only what is left to do. When something lands, delete it here (git is the history). Amend freely. Details of measured limits: `docs/loose-ends.md`.

**Goal:** an agent plays Link's Awakening DX through the engine. Shield and sword are obtained and the sword strikes through the `attack` skill; since flow-54 the harness learned clearable cells from observed results, contact-text leads, a lead leak guard, pending damage and heal reads, a scene-level lock settle, a hemmed probe, an attack chain, holes learned with a margin, a low-health margin, reachable-only objective candidates and non-object door hops (flows 55-69). The heavy tests run in CI with the encrypted ROM. Next: the first dungeon.

## Next, in order
1. **World model from anomalies and puzzle solving.** Today the harness replans around what surprised it, and the surprise is lost (a door that leads where the map says it should not, a wall that was open, an exit that is not where the screen geometry puts it). Make the surprise the signal.
   - (a) Prediction error: every action carries the outcome the world model expects (arrival place, position, open or blocked); every mismatch is logged as an anomaly and counted per place in `summary.json`, next to the existing symptoms (`left_with_unexhausted_people`, `damage_events`, `deaths`, `stuck_events`; still to add: partial maps, sign unread, unused item, repeated failure, cost drift).
   - (c) Generic experiment bench: the exit bench (`bench exits`, `data/exits.json`) tries each side of a puzzle place; generalize it to "action sequence -> observed effect" on keyframe forks (talk then walk, use an item on a person, a door cell, a side whose goto a person blocks: the left and right of 81/0 stay `unreached`). Results become facts with provenance and `verified_count`.
   - (d) Offline System 2 analyst (strong model, `koholint analyze <run>`): turns an anomaly with its texts and screenshots into testable hypotheses and experiments for the bench; proposals only, the agent may write data, never code. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-screen-protocol-and-improvement-loop-design.md`; level 3 (patch in a worktree, tests, keyframe-forked A/B) later.
   - (e) Hierarchical story objectives derived from in-game texts. A blocked sub-goal triggers an investigation (an experiment on the bench) instead of a detour. Progress on these sub-goals is the steering metric, next to places per dollar.
   - (f) Known cases chosen by the owner are tests of the loop itself: does the system detect the anomaly on its own, and in how many frames.
2. **Capability learning (brief: `docs/research/AUTO_IMPROVEMENT_BRIEF.md`, design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-capability-learning-slice.md`).** The loop works (`koholint learn`, `capability publish|retire`, `data/capabilities.json`) and `sword-on-object-92` is operational. What remains is the unreached learn trials: damage during a composite's goto, real walls and pockets, 4 `no_path` in 209/0 where the live grid disagrees with the sighting's, and the off-grid exit at the bottom of 208/0. The bench of 1(c) generalizes this loop beyond items on objects. Keep a narrow MetaPlanner for harness defects only. Measure game progress per 1000 frames and per dollar against a baseline fork, not the number of skills.
3. **Map screen as an objective source.** The manual says the map shows "!?" marks (important places) and "message" marks: read them deterministically; they feed the story objectives of 1(e). The map cursor address is unknown.
4. **Perception gaps.** Deterministic dialogue text decoding from VRAM first (dialogue is read as an image, then stitched; it feeds 1(e)), then Link's capabilities, `notable` events (so `last_notable` stops being empty), kind `object` of entities, item ids other than the shield and the sword (sword = 1 verified on 2 states).
5. **Finer navigation, interactions, combat.** Tile lattice and tile-level beliefs, exits straddling two cells; push, dive/swim, item use. A `goto` may step onto a walk-in exit cell already crossed and leave the place (a flow-84 `touch` on 171/10 did): plan around such cells, for instance with `@planner.avoid`. Combat tactics: hit-and-back, spin attack, groups of enemies, enemies the sword does not hurt; later, a cheap decision model for combat decision points, as an A/B against the main model.
6. **Save in the loop.** "Continue" after death is automatic (flow-22); add a `save_game` skill (needs the boot script to resume from the title after quitting and a check that the cartridge RAM changed; the four-button combination is not reachable through `press`).
7. **Viewer and live control.** Fork from the UI, pause/stop of the live engine; optional `--live-clock` with pause-on-danger and a reflex layer; background music (comfort, low priority: YouTube IFrame player, track per place kind via `data/music.json`, replay only); creatures overlay and map-cell thumbnails on hover; item icons captured from VRAM.

## Capabilities missing in run g6 (flow-104, runs d871 / 0e98 / 2c93 of KOHO-92, forked from abbe @94304)

On an hp drop, the entity in contact gets a `hostile` hypothesis with the hit size in typed data (verified at 2); a hostile type is never a talk target and counts as an enemy for heal and route rules.
- **`explore(direction)` goes another way.** The direction is only a tie-break inside the first non-empty candidate class: of 40 model calls with a direction, 31 took or aimed at another side (d871 7/10, 0e98 7/10, 2c93 17/20). 2c93 98221 in C7 (98/0) asks right toward D7 and leaves left into B7 (98500); d871 100978 in E7 (100/0) asks right toward F7 and goes up into E6 (101068), where it dies. A deterministic `travel(place)` over known edges and `leave(side)`; `explore(direction)` answers "no work on that side" instead.
- **A rock that answers with a text is not learned as blocked.** 2c93 in C7 (98/0): the push right from (4,1) into object 32 at (4,2) opens the "too heavy" text five times (97993, 98619, 99056, 99617, 100227), 228 to 288 frames each; each result reports `blocked_edges` (4,1,right) and the next walk plans through it again (`@too_heavy` is not set when the cell ahead is an object cell). - **A refuted lead still reads as advice.** lead-10, a game tip about entering a house, is printed as a plain tip (2c93 prompt 103837); 2c93 enters the hut of F7 (101/0) six times at 4 hp between 102886 and 103897, hp stays 4 (17 paid calls, $0.32), and 0e98 looks for a house to heal at 101711 and 101829. - **A corridor held by enemies the sword cannot reach.** Stub gate 7c9c (flow-106 at f03aac0, from respawn-171): on the heal walk from E6 (84/0) at 8 hp, C5 (66/0) entered from the right at (3,9), the only way to the known down exit (7,5) was held by types 27 and 11 at (4,6), which the grid read as objects; 7 attacks between 100226 and 100575 ended `no_path`, `blocked`, `out_of_reach` or `target_gone` save one kill, and the last cost 8 -> 4 hp. ## Open questions and unmeasured limits (one line each, details in `docs/loose-ends.md`)
- Zero-frame cap (flow-45): its thresholds are not measured: 3 identical results, at most 1 frame each, then a 120-frame wait. The key is place + skill + stop, so a model alternating two instant skills never trips it.
- Heal sources: `pending_heal` now reads a heal (the healing scene of 83/0 is measured); hearts picked up and enemy drops are still not inventoried.
- Does entering a house or cave heal? Not observed (hp stayed 4/24 in two houses for 2600 frames; caves untested): the `shelter` candidate was removed in flow-34 (no retreat at low hp).
- Routes over known places: an exit reachable from one landing of a place is assumed reachable from every landing except a pocket cell (one landing cell kept per side); in the scripted run from `sword-obtained` (flow-39) Link reached 192 in 1837 frames, found 192 up to 176 is a ledge, and then had no known route to 177-0 and no untried exit within reach (the bushes the sword may cut and the urchins are not frontiers yet).
- Loop watch (4 entries of a known place in 3600 frames without a new place) is measured on run `4ec5` only; on 21 older runs it would fire in 10 (mostly two screens crossed back and forth), whether all of them were loops is unchecked. The 225-0 contradiction of `4ec5` (row 4, col 5, up, blocked twice) is Link 4 px right of the cell centre beside the urchin of (3,6), not a map error (not replayed).
- Unmeasured thresholds: third of max hp, 3-cell danger range, 6-result stuck count, revisit caps (1800 / 2700 frames, 6 rounds, 3 stamps), autopilot chain caps (6 explores, 2400 frames), `untried_object` range, 2 failed objectives to refute a lead; a lead is `tested` when an objective that cited it was achieved, which says the objective was reached, not that the claim was true.
- `gap_entry` (-3..+4 px) is measured on one stairs cell; the escape from the 165/16 pocket is not replayed end to end.
- Addresses at `verified_count: 1` (`name_length`, `max_hearts`, `dialog_state`, `shield_box`, `gap_entry`, `link_frozen`, `rupees_hundreds`); `entity_state` (0xC290) and 0xDB47 are unexplained; item ids other than 4, counts other than rupees and upgrades are unread.
- Whether a run may inherit a map, a goal or leads from a previous run; ladder beyond the sword (goal text only, no milestone yet).
- People and dialogues: speaker attribution is a guess when two people overlap; stitching of scrolling pages and the exhaustion rule can miss a text given only on a third round; creature types 61, 220 and 47 are not settled; type 111 walks off its cell.
- Doors, signs, readiness, game modes and place kinds are measured on the overworld and houses only (caves, dungeons, holes, swimming, stairs are not); scripted scenes (`link_frozen`) are measured on the two scenes of 242/0 only.
- 0xC167 is unexplained: 15 through the whole sword scene, but 56 for 3646 frames of the intro (Link free at the end) and 1 for 95 frames in room 194 at frame 36951 of run 9ba1 with Link still and no other lock signal (not replayed); the wait after a lock (as long as the lock, up to 30 frames) rests on a 3-frame release after the sword scene and a 1-frame release after the owl of 128/0.
- The `scene` stop applies to every skill: a scene type not measured yet (holes, stairs, other characters arriving; the chest scene ends talk_to as its response) that holds Link could cut `explore` or `goto` short; `link_frozen` and the `frozen` mode rest on one run and one room (9ba1, 242/0) until another confirms them.

- A cut way (flow-84) lasts until a new context or a crossing from that landing, or (flow-89) until Link leaves a room where a stone was pushed: an opened switch or a goal route never lifts it, so a way that opens without an item or milestone stays cut for the rest of the context.

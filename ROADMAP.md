## Next, in order

2. **Skill `attack` (sword on A) and combat baseline.** Measure on emulator copies from the checkpoint: sword reach, damage, hit points per enemy type (9, 10, 197, 198, 14); face and align, strike, spin attack; reduce `Threat` margins per type instead of avoiding every zone; heal sources (hearts) still unmeasured, hp stays 8/24.
3. **Scripted scenes and unlisted pickups.** A dialogue or scene that starts (`scene_state`, `dialog_state`) means: stay and advance, never walk away (the sword needed about 1900 frames of dialogue after standing under entity 49 facing down, and the owl talks alone about 90 frames after arrival); offer a visible uncatalogued entity or object as a candidate (stand next to it, face it, A); the census (40 frames) misses dialogues that start later; `talk_to` must accept a reach cell when the target touches water.
4. **Lead statuses and the next goal.** Leads are only open claims: add open / tested / refuted with evidence. The goal now reads "go to the Enchanted Forest north of the village to meet the owl, then find the first dungeon"; open leads: a cave (Cave Flagello), "further east", "come see me in the forest", "wake the Wind Fish".
5. **Validation run from zero** (4-5 USD) once 1 to 4 are in; compare cost per room and places with the run of 2026-11-10 (`flow33`, 52,000 frames to reach room 240).
6. **Fast tests, keep them under 2 minutes.** `rake test` is about 1 min 55 s (CPU-bound: ~1400 s over 13 cores); the longest test (`delegated_engine_test` scripted run, boot plus verify from frame 0) is the floor. Next: share booted-ROM states between ROM tests, split `lib/koholint/policies/delegated.rb` (764 lines).
7. **Autonomous improvement loop toward the first dungeon (owner gave full autonomy).** Observe a run, read its symptoms (`summary.json` already counts `left_with_unexhausted_people`, `damage_events`, `deaths`, `stuck_events`; still to add: not ready, partial maps, sign unread, unused item, repeated failure, cost drift), decide harness change versus longer sessions, fix, relaunch (1-10 USD); then an offline analyst agent writing proposals (`koholint analyze <run>`). Level 3 (patch in a worktree, tests, keyframe-forked A/B) later. The agent may write more data, never code. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-screen-protocol-and-improvement-loop-design.md`.
8. **Capability learning loop (brief: `docs/research/AUTO_IMPROVEMENT_BRIEF.md`).** Smallest vertical slice: a new item or unknown interaction triggers a capability candidate (`hypothesis`, with provenance), validated by a bounded experiment on a fork from a checkpoint (needs item 1), published in a versioned registry (`validated` / `operational`, rollback) as a composition of existing primitives only, visible to the agent and traced on every use. Keep a narrow MetaPlanner for harness defects only. Measure game progress per 1000 frames and per dollar against a baseline fork, not the number of skills. Start with a short extension-point map and test plan; first candidate to try: the sword (`attack`, item 2) learned this way.
9. **Map screen as an objective source.** The manual says the map shows "!?" marks (important places) and "message" marks: read them deterministically. The map cursor address is unknown.
10. **Perception gaps.** Link's capabilities, deterministic dialogue text decoding from VRAM (dialogue is read as an image, then stitched), `notable` events (so `last_notable` stops being empty), kind `object` of entities, item ids other than the shield and the sword (sword = 1 verified on 2 states).
11. **Save in the loop.** "Continue" after death is automatic (flow-22, met live in run `20261105-221719-7000`: 5 deaths, run continued); add a `save_game` skill (needs the boot script to resume from the title after quitting and a check that the cartridge RAM changed; the four-button combination is not reachable through `press`).
12. **Finer navigation, interactions beyond walking, combat.** Tile lattice and tile-level beliefs, exits straddling two cells; push, dive/swim, item use; combat with a deterministic baseline.
13. **Viewer and live control.** Fork from the UI, pause/stop of the live engine; optional `--live-clock` with pause-on-danger and a reflex layer; background music (comfort, low priority: YouTube IFrame player, track per place kind via `data/music.json`, replay only); creatures overlay and map-cell thumbnails on hover; item icons captured from VRAM.
